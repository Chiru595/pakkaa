# Prior work

Pakkaa is inspired by my earlier project **PaidSafe** (https://github.com/Chiru595/paidsafe), a team chat room
where AI agents helped a small supplier decide whether to chase a late invoice, with code-enforced approvals and
a kill switch.

Pakkaa is a new product built for this hackathon from 21 Nov 2026 onward: a different platform (Nexconn), a new
web app and API, and new features for negotiating deals and approving payments. The commit history is the
record of what was built and when.

The author reviews all of it and can explain all of it.
